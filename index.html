Who I Am

I'm Ayman Odeh — a Mathematics with Economics student with a genuine interest in quantitative research and the way mathematical rigour can generate real edge in financial markets. I topped my cohort with a 94% average and hold regional medals in mathematics, which gives some context for the kind of problems I actually enjoy.

My work focuses on building rigorous, data-driven frameworks for portfolio construction, risk analysis, and asset allocation — grounded in financial theory and validated empirically rather than taken on faith. I've worked as an Investment Analyst on a real student-managed fund, and as a quantitative analyst at Porsche Cars GB, where I built pricing models and automated reporting pipelines that fed into board-level decisions.

Outside the models, I co-founded a health tech startup, speak three languages fluently, and spend time on boxing and chess — both teach you more about decision-making under uncertainty than they probably should. I'm drawn to roles where the maths is genuinely hard and the work actually matters.

Bayesian Black-Litterman with Machine-Learned Views
A research paper and full Python implementation that integrates machine learning return forecasts into the Black-Litterman portfolio construction framework. The methodology uses ML models to generate systematic investor "views" — replacing subjective analyst estimates with data-driven predictions — and feeds them into the Bayesian BL model to produce optimal portfolio weights. Validated with a comprehensive backtesting engine across an 11-asset multi-asset universe from 2010 to 2024, including performance attribution and drawdown analysis.

Stock Price Regime Detector
An interactive app that uses unsupervised machine learning — K-Means and Gaussian Mixture Models — to detect hidden market regimes in historical price data. Engineered five daily features (realised volatility, momentum, MA signal, rolling return, drawdown) and maps clusters to meaningful regime labels: Trending, Mean-Reverting, and High-Volatility. Includes an adaptive position-sizing strategy that responds to the detected regime, compared against a buy-and-hold benchmark.

Portfolio Risk Dashboard
An interactive portfolio analytics tool for custom equity and ETF portfolios. Enter any set of tickers, assign weights, and the dashboard pulls historical prices from Yahoo Finance to compute annualised return, volatility, Sharpe ratio, and max drawdown. Visualises cumulative portfolio performance, an asset correlation heatmap, and runs a Monte Carlo simulation across thousands of random long-only portfolios to map the efficient frontier — highlighting the max-Sharpe and min-volatility points.

Research & Papers

Original research at the intersection of machine learning, Bayesian statistics, and quantitative portfolio management.

2026 — Working Paper
Bayesian Black-Litterman with Machine-Learned Views: Integrating ML Forecasts into Portfolio Construction
This paper develops a systematic methodology for integrating machine learning return forecasts into the Black-Litterman portfolio construction framework. By replacing subjective analyst views with quantitative ML predictions, the approach derives a posterior distribution over expected returns that blends market equilibrium with learned signals. The framework is validated through a full Python backtesting implementation across an 11-asset multi-asset universe from 2010 to 2024, with comprehensive performance attribution and risk analysis throughout.

Hidden Markov Models for Market Regime Estimation and Regime-Conditional Portfolio Construction
Forthcoming research extending unsupervised regime detection towards probabilistic state modelling using Hidden Markov Models. Where clustering methods produce hard regime assignments, HMMs model the latent transition dynamics between states explicitly — enabling probabilistic regime inference and smoother strategy allocation. The framework will be validated across multi-asset universes with a focus on regime-conditional return distributions and drawdown characteristics. Connect on LinkedIn for updates.
